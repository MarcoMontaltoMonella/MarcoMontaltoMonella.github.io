About

A bit about me and my journey

I'm Marco Montalto Monella, a software engineer based in the San Francisco Bay Area. I grew up between France and Italy, surrounded by the Alps and the Mediterranean, before setting out on a path that would take me across continents.

My journey in tech started in England, where I began my career as a software engineer. From there, I moved to the United States, first studying at NYU in New York City, then heading west to California. I spent several years at Meta (formerly Facebook), working as a Core Data Performance Engineer, building and optimizing infrastructure at scale.

Today, I work at Pure Storage, where I continue to tackle challenges in storage infrastructure. Outside of work, I stay active with gymnastics, beach volleyball, and running. I believe in the power of motivation, determination, and collaboration to drive meaningful change.

I'm passionate about innovation, health, and the environment. If any of these topics resonate with you, I'd love to connect!
